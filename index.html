Before you open it — just so you know, this took a little courage to write.

🐯 — you know I'd never give you a lion, so here's a tigress instead.

Dear Nargiz,

Today you turn 25. It feels like a big number, but somehow it still isn't big enough to describe everything you are.

I hope this year brings you many good things. You work so hard at the Hilton in Astana, so I hope you also find time to rest, to breathe, and to enjoy small moments. I hope people around you see how special you are. And I hope you keep doing the things that make you happy, simply because they make you happy.

I still think about the evening I first saw you, in the botanical garden. You were playing tennis, and the light was soft around you. I did not plan to remember it so clearly. But I saw you, and I lost my head. It just happened, and I never wanted it to stop.

Whatever happens between us, and whatever the future brings — you will always be important to me. No matter what. And I am not sorry, not even a little, that I was there that evening, in that garden, watching you.

Happy birthday, Nargiz. I wish you all the best today, and always.

Merei
